Logistics

Getting there, dropping bags.

How to get there

Battersea Park, Bowling Green, London. Battersea Park and Queenstown Road rail stations are both a short walk away. Cycle racks and Santander docking nearby. If driving, plan ahead, as parking is limited.

What to bring & wear

  • Water: bring a refillable bottle.
  • Shoes: comfy running shoes are a must.
  • Clothes: weather-appropriate, breathable gear.
  • Bib: it's the only way we can time you.

Bag drop

Located at the Bowling Green. Please pack light — bag space is very limited, and it's strictly one bag per person.

Race bibs & safety

  • Your bib is your ID and your timer, so do not forget it.
  • Pin it to the front of your shirt.
  • Fill in the emergency info on the back. This is required.
  • No bib? Collect one at the Bowling Green on the day. Expect queues, so come early.
